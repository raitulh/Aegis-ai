# Code of Conduct

We want DataBattles — the codebase and every community running on it — to be welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive, credit others' work, assume good faith, respect privacy, and follow
competition rules (no private code sharing between teams, no multiple accounts, no leaderboard probing).

**Unacceptable:** harassment, discrimination, sexualized content, threats, doxxing, plagiarism, cheating, spam, and
misrepresenting affiliations, results or endorsements.

**Enforcement:** moderators may hide or remove content, issue warnings, suspend or ban accounts, and disqualify
competition entries. Decisions are recorded in the audit log. Report issues through the in-app *Report* action or to
the operator's contact address. Appeals go to the platform administrators.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
