# Moderator guide

* **Moderation queue** groups open reports by target (threads, comments, projects, datasets, users, competitions) with
  the number of reports, reasons, excerpts and the owner's account status. Reporters are never revealed to owners.
* Actions (a note is always required and written to the audit log): *dismiss*, *warn*, *hide*, *restore*, *delete*,
  *take down* (projects/datasets), *freeze* (competitions), *suspend owner*. Owners are notified of the outcome.
* Account status: moderators can suspend and restore; bans and changes to staff accounts require a platform admin.
  Suspension revokes all sessions immediately.
* In discussions you can lock, pin, hide threads and hide comments; edit histories are available for review.
* Competition organizers moderate their own competition forums; escalate platform-level issues to admins.
* Follow the [community guidelines](/guidelines); be consistent, explain decisions, and prefer the least severe action
  that resolves the problem.
