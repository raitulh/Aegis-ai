# Sponsor guide

* Sponsor organizations are created like any organization (type *Sponsor*); organizers attach you to competitions with a
  tier and a short blurb, which appear on the competition page.
* **Sponsor dashboard** (organization page → Sponsor dashboard): sponsored events with participants, teams, submissions
  and views, plus totals.
* **Talent list**: only participants who switched on *Open to opportunities* in their profile appear, with their public
  profile, best rank in your sponsored events and public skills. Emails are never shared — reach out through the links
  people publish on their profiles or via the organizers.
* Sponsor announcements submitted to a competition are reviewed by its organizers before publication.
* Do not present seeded demo data as real engagement; demo items carry a "Demo data" label.
