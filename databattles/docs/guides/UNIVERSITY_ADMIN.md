# University admin guide

## Verification

1. Create the organization (type *University*) and fill in the public profile.
2. **Admin → Settings → Request verification** with evidence (official website, contact). Platform admins review it.
3. Platform admins set the institutional **email domains** (e.g. `cs.example.edu`). Public email providers are refused.

Once verified, students can prove membership by confirming an address on those domains. Such memberships show as
*verified* on profiles; everything else a user types about their university is labelled *self-declared*.

## Members

* **Requests**: approve or decline with an optional private note (the member never sees it).
* **Invites**: email-bound (single use) or shareable links with a max-use count and expiry. You cannot invite to a role at
  or above your own; only owners can create admins.
* **Roles**: owner (full control), admin (institution management), manager (content: competitions, courses, datasets),
  member. The last owner cannot leave or be demoted.
* **Departments** help students self-organize and power department breakdowns.
* Roster CSV export contains handles, names, roles, departments and verification method — never personal emails.

## Dashboard

Active/verified/pending members, joins per month, hosted competitions with member participation, course completions,
department counts, and top performers — the latter only for members who allow their university to be shown next to their
results. Groups smaller than three are not broken out.

## Plans

The *Plan* page shows the current plan and entitlements. Billing is manual in this deployment; contact the platform team.
